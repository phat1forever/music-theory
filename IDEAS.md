# Future ideas

One place for things the app could do next. Add new ideas under the section they fit, one line
each with a short reason. When an idea is built, delete it from here.

## Planned

- **Tabs from audio.** Upload an audio file or play into the microphone, detect the notes in the
  browser, and write them out as guitar tab, keyboard notes and sheet music. Works best on
  single-note lines (melodies, riffs, solos) from a clean recording. Pasting a streaming or video
  link, or transcribing full band mixes, would need a backend service; the app has none today.

## Lessons and content

- **Hands-together scales** (keyboard): parallel and contrary motion. Raised by the advanced
  pianist tester as the biggest remaining gap in the keyboard track.
- **Sustain pedal lesson** (keyboard): the waltz and broken-chord left-hand patterns want it.
- **Pieces where both hands move independently** (keyboard): the left hand mostly holds whole
  notes or block chords at present.
- **Harmonic minor at the keyboard**: it is taught in theory but never played as a scale.
- **Mixing major and minor pentatonic over a blues** (guitar), with the ♭3 to 3 move.
- **Arpeggio shapes inside the pentatonic boxes** (guitar): targeting 3rds, not only roots.
- **More solo studies** (guitar): a slow blues, a minor blues, a study in boxes 2 to 5.
- **Bend variations** (guitar): bend and release, unison bends, double stops.
- **Turnaround licks and call-and-response drills** (guitar).
- **Variations on the shuffle rhythm pattern** (guitar): 6th on the off-beat, the 5-6-♭7-6 walk.
- **More theory**: inversions and slash chords, cadences, time signatures beyond 3/4 and 4/4,
  pickup bars, dynamics and articulation.
- **A week-one practice schedule** for complete beginners.
- **Photos or short clips** of hand position, posture and common fixes (buzzing strings, sore
  fingers).

## Practice and grading

- **Playing-based placement check.** Placement is one multiple-choice question per unit, which
  can skip material a player has not really mastered.
- **Quizzes that must be answered** before moving on (they can be skipped today).
- **Loop a section while playing in time**, not only while listening.
- **Record yourself and listen back.**
- **Bend target check**: listen for the pitch a bend reaches, not only its starting note.
- **Rhythm-only drills**: tap or strum a rhythm with no pitch checking.
- **Cap or flag self-rated tempos** set far above an exercise's goal.

## Notation

- **Chord fingerings stacked at each notehead**, not written in a row above the chord.
- **8va marking for high guitar parts**, to avoid stacks of ledger lines at the 12th fret.
- **Double-sharp and double-flat symbols** in text labels (the staff already uses them).
- **Tie rendering on narrow screens**: the second half of a tie across a line break is cramped.
- **Final double bar line** at the end of a piece.

## Explore and Chords

- **Keyboard chord voicings**: one hand position with fingering and inversions, in place of every
  chord tone marked across three octaves.
- **Option to show the keyboard before the fretboard** for keyboard-first players.
- **More chord types**: 11ths, 13ths, altered dominants beyond 7♯9 and 7♭9.
- **Slash chords kept on a chord sheet** with their bass note in the name.
- **More voicings in open tunings** (DADGAD currently offers one shape for some chords).
- **Print or export a chord sheet.**
- **Song suggestions** for the chords a player already knows.

## Jam

- **Keyboard-first Jam view**: left-hand voicings and comping patterns, with the guitar neck
  hidden.
- **More styles**: slow blues in 12/8, reggae, bossa nova, a ballad.
- **Adjustable backing**: mute drums or bass, change the feel, pick the number of choruses.
- **Prompts while playing**: "rest this bar", "answer the last phrase".

## Ear training

- **Chord quality and scale recognition**, alongside the interval trainer.
- **Harmonic and descending intervals**, with reference-song hints.
- **Rhythm dictation.**

## App

- **Sync progress between devices.** Progress lives in one browser today; this would need
  accounts and a backend.
- **Export and import progress** as a file, as a no-backend alternative.
- **Installable app (PWA)** with offline use.
- **Sampled instrument sounds** in place of the synthesised guitar and piano.
- **Custom tunings in lessons and practice**, not only on the Chords page.
